# RAHAT Presentation Demo Script

This script is designed for a 3-5 minute live demonstration of the RAHAT internal-round prototype.

## Introduction (1 min)
"Good morning Judges. We are presenting RAHAT, a Flood Hazard-Based Red-Zone Identification and Emergency Response Optimization System built for Dhemaji District, Assam."
"RAHAT is not a prediction model. It is an operational command tool that ingests live satellite data and translates it into physical response plans."

## Step 1: Temporal Flood View (1 min)
*Open the Dashboard. Ensure the 11 Jul Baseline is selected.*
"Here we see the pre-flood baseline of the Jonai-Murkongselek cluster."
*Click 18 Jul Onset*
"As the monsoon intensifies, Sentinel-1 SAR imagery detects the onset of the flood. We process this raw satellite backscatter to extract the exact inundation footprint."
*Click 22 Jul Active*
"By July 22nd, we see the active peak of the flood."

## Step 2: Hazard and Habitations (1 min)
*Toggle 'Show Hazard Zones'*
"We do not just overlay water on a map. We combine the flood extent with bare-earth elevation and slope gradients to generate a 4-tier Hazard Map. This is a transparent physics-based score, not a black-box AI."
*Point to the map*
"The red zones are actively flooded, low-elevation, flat terrains where water pools dangerously."
*Point to the Side Panel*
"The system intersects this hazard layer with habitation data. We instantly see that 10 habitations and nearly 6,000 people are affected."

## Step 3: Optimization and Road Closure (2 min)
*Point to the 'Top Priority Missions' table*
"Our needs engine identifies the top priority villages based on their red-zone exposure and population."
*Point to the 'Optimization & Routing' panel*
"For the highest priority mission, RAHAT calculates the optimal deployment route from the Jonai Response Base, leveraging the official Government Resource Baseline—such as the SDRF Motorised Boats shown at the bottom."
*Click 'Simulate Road Closure'*
"In floods, roads wash away. If the primary route is submerged, a commander simply clicks here. RAHAT instantly re-optimizes the logistical graph, finds the next-best alternative route, and shows exactly how many hours of delay this will cause."

## Conclusion
"RAHAT bridges the gap between complex space-tech and on-the-ground disaster response, providing explainable, actionable intelligence to the Ministry of Home Affairs."
